# Cydetix v0.6.0-alpha.12

Cydetix `v0.6.0-alpha.12` completes the final major alpha hardening and beta-readiness work on top
of the immutable `v0.6.0-alpha.11` release.

This release improves operator-facing explanations, validates the eleven supported agent adapters,
expands deterministic corpus evidence, and hardens bounded analysis while preserving Cydetix's
proof-state semantics, remediation authority, and fail-closed treatment of incomplete evidence.

## Human output and explainability

Human-readable output now shows finding locations, independent proof dimensions, useful `UNKNOWN`
causes, remediation ceilings, verification state, and next actions while preserving distinct
findings at a shared source location.

`cydetix explain` now defaults to readable text, with `--format json` retaining structured output.
`cydetix status` provides a read-only view of local integration status. Terminal output remains
bounded; complete structured JSON and SARIF evidence remain available.

## MCP and agent integration

The MCP server now enforces its declared argument types, rejects unknown keys, and bounds request
buffering before a newline arrives. The public MCP surface remains exactly:

- `cydetix_scan`
- `cydetix_fix`
- `cydetix_explain`

Configuration, adapter, and direct subprocess validation passed for Codex, Claude, Cursor, Gemini,
Cline, Roo, Continue, Copilot, Goose, Windsurf, and generic MCP integrations. This does not
establish universal live-host behavior: representative Codex behavior was exercised, while broader
live-host validation remains incomplete where hosts or authentication were unavailable.

## Conservative proof and analysis hardening

Three previously false insecure conclusions were corrected to conservative retained `UNKNOWN`
observations. Password-adjacent fast hashes and private-key headers remain observations rather than
proof of insecure storage or deployed key material when the required purpose or payload evidence is
missing.

Babel scope failures are contained to the affected file. Application propagation, evidence-path, and
recursive identity-propagation exhaustion fail closed with explicit counters and `TRUNCATED`
coverage. Existing AST and application limits remain in force, and incomplete propagated trust is
discarded rather than promoted to a secure conclusion.

## Deterministic corpus evidence

Alpha.12 expands the pinned corpus to 30 immutable public repository revisions. Every target
completed two normalized offline scans with determinism passing. The evidence retains findings,
proof instances, `UNKNOWN` results, completeness, resource limits, fingerprints, timings, memory,
and adjudication scope.

Corpus ground truth remains selective and incomplete. No corpus-wide recall or accuracy claim is
made. One independently adjudicated lexical false-positive observation remains recorded, and
`UNKNOWN` and `TRUNCATED` results remain part of the evidence. Thirteen corpus repositories were
`TRUNCATED` under documented resource or application bounds.

## Performance evidence

Comparative measurements cover representative small, medium, and large targets with semantic
equivalence retained. The initial FastAPI tail measurement showed a p95 increase, but the repeated
paired-cluster result did not consistently reproduce it and its interval includes both 1.0 and the
original ratio.

FastAPI tail-performance evidence therefore remains `INCONCLUSIVE`. It is not a demonstrated
material regression, a proven improvement, or a non-inferiority result.

## Readiness result

The recorded Alpha.12 verdict is:

`ALPHA12_BETA_READY_WITH_LIMITATIONS`

The readiness evidence retains required local and hosted gates, deterministic corpus results,
public-contract review, and unchanged remediation authority. It does not claim complete
vulnerability coverage or convert missing evidence into a passing security conclusion. The readiness
validator permits explicitly documented non-blocking limitations while continuing to reject
demonstrated regressions, unresolved supported-pattern false negatives, unreviewed findings, missing
mandatory gates, and contradictory or incomplete readiness evidence.

## Security boundaries and limitations

- Broader live-host validation is incomplete where hosts or authentication were unavailable.
- FastAPI tail-performance evidence is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not a demonstrated regression or proven
  improvement.
- Corpus ground truth is selective and incomplete.
- No corpus-wide recall or accuracy claim is permitted.
- One adjudicated lexical false-positive observation remains recorded.
- `UNKNOWN` and `TRUNCATED` semantics remain unchanged; 13 corpus repositories were `TRUNCATED`
  under documented resource or application bounds.
- Absence of a finding is not proof of complete vulnerability coverage.

## Compatibility and authority

Alpha.12 preserves the four proof states:

- `PROVEN_SECURE`
- `PROVEN_INSECURE`
- `UNKNOWN`
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`

Confidence, reachability, completeness, and verification remain independent dimensions. Remediation
authority remains monotonic, and the existing exact session-cookie transformation remains the only
SAFE adapter. No universal agent-host validation, zero-false-positive guarantee, corpus-wide recall,
or complete vulnerability coverage is claimed.
