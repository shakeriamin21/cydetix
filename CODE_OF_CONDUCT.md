# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be constructive, assume good
intent while evaluating technical claims rigorously, respect privacy and disclosure boundaries, and
avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or personally harassing behavior.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainers through the contact method in the repository profile. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical; retaliation is not tolerated.

Security disagreements should be resolved with reproducible evidence, clearly stated assumptions,
and respect for affected users—not personal attacks or public disclosure of unresolved
vulnerabilities.
